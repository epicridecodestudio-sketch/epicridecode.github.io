Arrowflow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

EpicRideCode (“we,” “us,” or “our”) made Arrowflow, a puzzle game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use Arrowflow, including through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), Firebase Analytics, and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). The information collected by these services can depend on their versions and settings.

Information the game handles

Arrowflow does not ask you to create an account. Game content is processed on your device. The game stores your music on/off preference and applicable advertising consent choices locally on your device.

Advertising and analytics

Arrowflow uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to show ads and Google Analytics for Firebase to understand app use and improve the game. Their SDKs may collect and share data with Google, such as IP address or coarse location derived from it, app interactions and lifecycle events, diagnostics, device and advertising identifiers, and an app-instance identifier. AdMob may use this information for advertising, ad measurement, analytics, and fraud prevention. Firebase Analytics may use it for usage measurement and reporting. The exact data depends on the SDK versions, features, and settings used in the app.

Google’s User Messaging Platform displays consent messages before ads are requested and provides privacy options when required. You can manage available ad and privacy choices through the in-app privacy options when shown. You can also reset or limit use of your Android Advertising ID in your device settings.

Google processes information under its own privacy terms. You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy.

Retention and choices

Your music preference remains in the app’s local storage until you change it or clear the app’s data. Data handled by Google’s advertising and analytics services is subject to Google’s retention and privacy controls. We do not receive account details from Arrowflow because the game has no account feature.

Children’s privacy

Arrowflow is not designed to collect a child’s personal information directly. If the game is configured or distributed for children, we will use ad and analytics settings and services that comply with applicable children’s privacy requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game’s features or data practices change. The revised policy will be posted at this address with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact epicridecode@gmail.com.
